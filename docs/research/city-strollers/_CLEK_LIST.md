## How compatibility works (Clek's stated mechanism)

- The Liing / Liingo shell uses the **"Maxi-Cosi–style" universal infant-seat
  adapter footprint** (shared with Nuna and Cybex). It attaches to any listed
  stroller via **an adapter made by that stroller's own brand**.
- **Exception — UPPAbaby:** Clek makes its **own dedicated adapter** for the
  UPPAbaby **Vista (V1/V2/V3)** and **Cruz (V1/V2/V3)** —
  <https://clekinc.com/products/car-seat-adapter-for-uppababy-vista-and-cruz>.
- Clek's inclusion bar: each listed stroller was "tested with the Clek
  Liing/Liingo infant seat (by either a certified independent testing body
  and/or the stroller manufacturer) and is compliant with the applicable ASTM
  standards for car seats on strollers."
- **Disclaimer (Clek):** "Due to ongoing supply chain challenges, we recommend
  checking with the stroller manufacturer to confirm adapter availability."
