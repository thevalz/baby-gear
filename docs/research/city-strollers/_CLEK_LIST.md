## Clek's stated mechanism & disclaimers (verbatim intent)

- "The Liing and Liingo are compatible with the following strollers **using the
  Maxi-Cosi® car seat adapter(s) available for each stroller.**"
- "Strollers on this list have been **tested** with the Liing infant seat (by
  either a certified independent testing body and/or the stroller manufacturer)
  and are compliant with the applicable **ASTM standards** for car seats on
  strollers."
- **Important Note:** "Please make sure to confirm adapter availability either
  directly with the adapter manufacturer, or at your preferred retailer."
- Footer: "All stroller trademarks … are proprietary trademarks of the stroller
  brand owners and not Clek." + "due to ongoing supply chain challenges, we
  recommend checking with the stroller manufacturer to confirm adapter
  availability."

> **Adapter maker:** in almost every case the adapter is made by the **stroller
> brand** (the Maxi-Cosi-style footprint). The exception is **UPPAbaby CRUZ &
> Vista**, which use **"Clek car seat adapters for UPPAbaby"** (Clek-made).
> Note UPPAbaby **Minu** and **Ridge** use **UPPAbaby-made** Maxi-Cosi adapters,
> NOT the Clek adapter.

## 🚩 Correction this list forces on existing study data

Clek's own definitive list shows **B.O.B. Gear Alterrain / Alterrain Pro is
compatible with the Liing/Liingo** via BOB's **Single Infant Car Seat Adapter
(S12046000)**. The existing car-seat study
([`docs/research/car-seats/clek-liing.json`](../car-seats/clek-liing.json) and
`seed.json`) currently records `fitsAlterrain: false` ("genuinely on neither BOB
list"). **Per Clek's authoritative list, that is wrong** — the owned Clek Liing
*does* fit the owned BOB Alterrain Pro. Flag for the integration pass:
flip `clek-liing.fitsAlterrain → true`, cite adapter S12046000, and re-score the
Liing's `compat`. (Wayfinder is genuinely NOT on Clek's list — only Alterrain /
Alterrain Pro and Revolution Flex 3.0 are.) Leave the actual `seed.json` edit for
the integration pass / owner confirmation since it revises prior committed
research.
